Pinterest Pack — how to use it
==============================
You have 78 ready-to-pin images (pins/) and pins.csv with a title, description, link, and
suggested board for each. Pinterest is a slow burn — pins keep sending traffic for years.

SETUP (once):
1. Use a free Pinterest BUSINESS account (needed for analytics + rich pins).
2. Create these boards (matches the "Suggested board" column):
     Santa Cruz Boardwalk · Sunset Wallpapers · Santa Cruz Nature · Santa Cruz Coast
3. In Pinterest settings, "Claim" your domain storymodeguy.com so pins credit you.

POSTING:
- Create a Pin → upload the image from pins/ → paste the Title and Description from pins.csv →
  set the Destination link (all go to your prints/wallpapers) → pick the Suggested board.
- Pace it: ~5-10 pins/day, not all at once. 78 pins = ~2 weeks of steady pinning.
- Best times: evenings & weekends. Re-pin your top performers to other boards later.

TIP: link some pins to specific products instead of #prints if you want — e.g. the
Complete Wallpaper Collection (payhip.com/b/uIdFV). Ask Claude to regenerate the CSV with
per-product links anytime.
