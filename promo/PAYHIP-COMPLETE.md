A big, self-contained product compiled from the full-resolution photography (Google Drive originals):

## The files
The full bundle is `promo/packs/smg-complete-wallpaper-collection.zip` (~97 MB) with two folders inside:
`Desktop-4K/` (45 × 3840×2160) and `Phone/` (50 × 1080×2340), plus a README.

Because chat has a 30 MB limit, I've delivered it as **4 parts**:
- `collection-desktop-4k-part1.zip`, `part2.zip`, `part3.zip` (the 45 desktop 4K wallpapers)
- `collection-phone.zip` (the 50 phone wallpapers)

**To upload to Payhip, either:**
- **Option A (simplest):** create one product and attach all 4 zips as its files — Payhip lets a product carry multiple files, and the buyer gets them all. Or
- **Option B (one file):** extract the 4 zips into a single folder so you have `Desktop-4K/` + `Phone/` together, re-zip that folder, and upload the single zip.
