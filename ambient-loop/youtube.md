### On the AI disclosure

YouTube requires disclosing realistic synthetic content, and photorealistic
AI-generated nature footage is squarely in scope. Tick "Altered or synthetic
content" in the upload flow, and keep the line in the description. It is a
small label, not a demotion — and misdeclaring risks a strike on the channel.

### On the silence

Ambient videos on YouTube usually carry audio, and viewers arriving without
warning will bounce and say so in the comments. Two honest ways to handle it:

1. **Keep it silent, and say so everywhere** — title, thumbnail, first line of
   the description. There is a real audience that specifically wants a silent
   screen. That is what this kit is written for.
2. **Add an audio track before uploading.** A rain-and-forest bed would suit
   this footage. If you supply a track I can loop it to length and mux it in —
   no re-encode of the video, so no quality loss. Then drop "No Music" from the
   title, thumbnail and description.

Option 2 will almost certainly reach more people. Option 1 is the tighter product.
