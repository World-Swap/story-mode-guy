# Market Research — High-Demand Drone Guides ($20–50 PDFs)

**Prepared:** August 2026 · For: Story Mode Guy
**Goal:** Identify the most-searched drone-flying and drone-video help topics with proven paid demand, then produce a sellable PDF guide line ($17–39 + bundle) — mirroring the photography and videography lines already live, and tying directly into the video line ("how to put drone shots in your videos").
**Method:** Same framework — convergent signal from SEO/keyword patterns, beginner-question roundups, and live paid products (Amazon KDP, Gumroad, Udemy/Skillshare, YouTube creator products, Pilot Institute-style courses) with observed price points.

---

## How to read this
Each topic is scored on three signals:
1. **Search demand** — how often people look this up.
2. **Paid demand** — is someone already *selling* this, and at what price?
3. **Buyer motivation** — urgency ("can I legally fly here?"), fear ("how do I not crash a $1,000 drone?"), or ROI ("get paid to fly") convert far better than "nice to know."

> **Data caveat:** Keyword tools block automated access, so exact monthly-volume integers aren't all extractable. Rankings rest on convergent qualitative signal + live price points. Validate the shortlist in Google Keyword Planner / Ahrefs before scaling.

## Why drones are a strong third line
The drone buyer is often the **same creator** who bought the photo and video guides — they just added a $500–2,000 flying camera and are nervous about two things: **crashing it** and **breaking the law** with it. That's unusually strong buyer motivation (fear + legal urgency), and it sits right next to the video line: aerial shots are useless until you can *edit them into a story*, which is exactly the cross-sell. High gear spend also means high willingness-to-pay for guidance.

> **Accuracy note for the legal guide:** Drone law is US-FAA-centric in this line, changes over time, and varies by country. That guide is written around durable fundamentals (registration thresholds, recreational vs. Part 107, LAANC, controlled airspace, altitude limits) and **how to check current rules**, with a clear "verify with your aviation authority before every flight" disclaimer — not exact fees or menu paths that change.

## Recommended line — the top 10 (parallel to the photo/video lines)

Spread walks a pilot through the whole journey — **fly → legal → shoot → settings → edit → integrate → income → stills → safety → reference** — so guides cross-sell each other and the bundle. Chosen over #11–13 because FPV needs different gear, and business/mapping are narrower niches (great future add-ons).

### 1. Drone Flying for Beginners — *$27*
- **Search:** "how to fly a drone / first drone flight" — top-tier evergreen.
- **Buyer:** Someone who just unboxed their first drone and is nervous to take off.
- **Covers:** the controls and flight modes, the pre-flight checklist, calibration and GPS lock, your first hover and basic maneuvers, reading the app/telemetry, battery and range basics, and landing confidently.

### 2. Drone Laws, Registration & Part 107 — *$34*
- **Search:** "do I need to register my drone / where can I fly / Part 107" — huge, urgent, high-intent.
- **Paid:** High willingness-to-pay because it removes fear of fines and unlocks paid work.
- **Buyer:** Anyone unsure what's legal — and anyone who wants to fly commercially.
- **Covers:** recreational rules and the knowledge test, the registration threshold, controlled airspace and LAANC, where you can and can't fly, altitude and line-of-sight rules, night/over-people basics, and the Part 107 path to flying for money — framed US/FAA with a "verify locally" disclaimer.

### 3. Cinematic Drone Moves — *$29*
- **Search:** "cinematic drone shots / drone moves" — very strong creator search.
- **Buyer:** Pilot whose footage is technically fine but boring.
- **Covers:** the core moves (reveal, orbit/point-of-interest, flyover, top-down, dronie, fly-through, tracking), when to use each, smooth stick control, speed and altitude for cinematic feel, and pairing moves with the scene.

### 4. Drone Camera Settings for Video — *$27*
- **Search:** "drone camera settings / drone ND filters" — steady.
- **Buyer:** Footage looks juddery, over-sharp, or flat.
- **Covers:** frame rate and the shutter rule in the air, ND filters (the aerial must-have), resolution/bitrate, flat/D-Log profiles, white balance, and exposure with the drone's tools.

### 5. Editing & Grading Drone Footage — *$29*
- **Search:** "edit drone footage / color grade drone video."
- **Buyer:** Aerial clips that won't match the rest of the edit.
- **Covers:** organizing aerial clips, stabilizing and fixing rolling shutter/jello, speed ramps and reveals in the edit, color-correcting and grading aerials, matching air to ground footage, and export.

### 6. Add Drone Shots to Your Videos — *$27*
- **Search:** "how to use drone footage in videos" — **under-served** as a paid product; the direct bridge to your video line.
- **Buyer:** Has great aerial clips but the video still feels like a slideshow.
- **Covers:** aerial as b-roll, the establishing-shot job, cutting between ground and air, motivated transitions (match-cut on motion), pacing and music, and building a sequence with a beginning/middle/end.

### 7. Real Estate & Property Drone Video — *$39* (income)
- **Search:** "real estate drone / property aerial video" — high buyer intent, income ROI.
- **Paid:** Highest WTP (parallels the $39 real-estate photo and video guides) because it pays for itself and pairs with them.
- **Buyer:** Side-hustlers, agents, hosts.
- **Covers:** the legal must-dos for paid property work, the exterior/lot shot list, smooth reveal and orbit of a home, altitude and privacy, pairing with ground video, a fast delivery edit, and what to charge.

### 8. Drone Photography (Aerial Stills) — *$29*
- **Search:** "drone photography / aerial photos" — strong, distinct from video.
- **Buyer:** Wants striking stills, not just clips.
- **Covers:** top-down composition, panoramas and stitching, HDR/bracketing for high-contrast scenes, shooting RAW, golden-hour aerials, and a quick aerial editing pass.

### 9. Fly Safe: Avoid Crashes & Flyaways — *$24*
- **Search:** "drone flyaway / how to not crash a drone" — **fear-driven, high-intent.**
- **Buyer:** "I'm terrified of losing a $1,000 drone."
- **Covers:** the top crash causes, wind and weather limits, GPS/compass and interference, battery discipline, Return-to-Home set up right, obstacle avoidance and its blind spots, signal loss, and a pre-flight safety checklist.

### 10. The Drone Pilot's Cheat-Sheet Pack — *$17* (add-on)
- **Search:** printable "pre-flight checklist / drone settings" sells steadily on Etsy.
- **Buyer:** New pilot; impulse add-on that lifts order value.
- **Covers:** printable field cards — pre-flight and post-flight checklists, camera-settings and ND quick-set, a legal quick-reference, the cinematic-moves shot list, a wind/weather go-no-go card, and an emergency (flyaway/signal-loss) card.

**Bundle — The Complete Drone Library (all 10) — ~$99** (vs. $282 à la carte). Anchors value and lifts order size, same as the photo/video bundles.

## Pricing rationale
Drone buyers spend heavily on hardware, so WTP is high. Spread: one $17 add-on, a core cluster at $24–29, two premium at $34–39 (legal/Part 107 and real-estate income), and a ~$99 bundle to anchor. Undercuts the $150–300 drone courses while out-designing the $5 Kindle ebooks.
