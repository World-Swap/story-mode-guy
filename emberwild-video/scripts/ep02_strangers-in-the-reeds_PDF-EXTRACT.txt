EMBERWILD — EPISODE TWO: STRANGERS IN THE REEDS
Tagline: "we thought we were the last."
Source: EMBERWILD_Episode2_illustrated-comic.pdf (Google Drive / EMBERWILD - FINAL)
Extracted verbatim. Balloon order within a page is layout order, not reading order.

FRONT MATTER
  They had walked west until the world ran out of names.
  Fire behind them. Ahead — water, and reeds, and something that watched from the far
  bank and did not run.
  The Vael believed themselves the last of their kind.

p1  CAPTION: No one had slept.
    BRAM: IT'S STILL DOWN THERE. I SAW THE WATER MOVE AN HOUR AGO.
    BRAM: A VAEL WITH A SNAKE. COILED ROUND ITS NECK LIKE IT BELONGED THERE. THAT'S NOT NATURAL.
    ROOK: NEITHER'S A HORNED CAT THAT WALKS OUT OF A FIRE. TO THEM, MAYBE WE'RE THE WRONG ONES.
    ROOK: IT DIDN'T ATTACK. IT WATCHED US AND LEFT. THAT'S NOT A HUNTER.
    ROOK: WE NEED WATER. WE NEED TO KNOW WHAT'S AHEAD. AND I NEED TO KNOW IF WE'RE REALLY ALONE.
    BRAM: YOU'RE GOING DOWN THERE.

p2  YARROW: THE OLD STORIES SAY THE VAEL SCATTERED, LONG AGO. TO WATER, TO STONE, TO SKY.
            I THOUGHT THEY WERE ONLY STORIES TO PUT CHILDREN TO SLEEP.
    YARROW: IF ONE OF THOSE STORIES IS STANDING ON THAT RIVERBANK — THEN SOME OF THE OTHERS
            ARE TRUE TOO. AND NOT ALL OF THEM ARE GENTLE.
    SORREL: I'M COMING.
    ROOK: NO.
    SORREL: YOU SAID THE BOND ONLY CHOOSES THE HONEST. IF THEY'RE VAEL, THEY'LL TRUST HONEST
            FASTER THAN THEY TRUST A CHIEF WITH A KNIFE.
    ROOK: ...YARROW. IS SHE RIGHT?
    YARROW: THE CHILD IS USUALLY RIGHT. IT'S VERY ANNOYING.

p3  CAPTION: The marsh swallowed sound. Their own footsteps went quiet.
    SORREL: ROOK. THE WATER. THINGS ARE IN IT.
    ROOK: KEEP YOUR HANDS WHERE THEY CAN SEE YOURS.
    ROOK: THERE.
    CAPTION: Ahead, half-seen in the mist and the reeds: a figure. Still. Watching. And around
             its shoulders, a long sinuous coil catching the light.

p4  CAPTION: SEDGE. Lean, wet-slick hair, marsh-tribe teal and grey. No weapon — and no need of one.
    CAPTION: Cat and serpent. Two bonded souls, sizing each other up. Neither moved.
    SEDGE: YOU'RE LOUD. ALL THREE OF YOU. THE CAT ESPECIALLY. I HEARD YOU COMING SINCE THE TREELINE.
    SEDGE: FIRE-PEOPLE. YOU SMELL LIKE ASH AND YOU DON'T KNOW HOW TO WALK IN WATER.

p5  ROOK: WE ARE FIRE-PEOPLE. OUR HOME BURNED. WE CAME WEST WITH THE SMOKE.
    SEDGE: EVERYONE'S HOME BURNS EVENTUALLY. WHY BRING YOUR TROUBLE TO MY WATER?
    ROOK: BECAUSE I DIDN'T KNOW YOUR WATER WAS YOURS. I THOUGHT WE WERE THE LAST VAEL ALIVE.
    SEDGE: ...SAY THAT AGAIN.
    ROOK: THE LAST. I THOUGHT MY PEOPLE WERE THE LAST VAEL IN THE WORLD.
    SEDGE: AND HERE YOU ARE. STANDING IN MY REEDS. SMELLING OF ASH. NOT THE LAST.
    SEDGE: THAT'S WHAT THE MARSH-MOTHER SAYS. EVERY WINTER. "WE ARE THE LAST. HOLD TOGETHER,
           BECAUSE THERE IS NO ONE ELSE."
    ROOK: OUR KEEPER SAYS THE SAME. DIFFERENT WORDS. SAME FIRE.

p6  SORREL: I ONLY BONDED THREE NIGHTS AGO. THIS IS MOTH. IS YOURS — IS RILL A SHE? CAN I —
    SEDGE: KEEP YOUR HANDS WHERE RILL CAN SEE THEM. AND TELL YOUR CAT TO STOP LOOKING AT ME
           LIKE DINNER.
    CAPTION: The cub, half-asleep, booped noses with the serpent. Neither bothered by what
             their humans feared.
    SEDGE: THE BOND DOESN'T CARE WHAT SHAPE THE OTHER HALF TAKES. IT JUST WANTS THE HALVES
           BACK TOGETHER.
    SEDGE: ...THE MARSH-MOTHER WILL WANT TO SEE YOU. SHE WON'T WANT TO. BUT SHE'LL WANT TO.

p7  CAPTION: There was a path. You could only see it if you already knew it was there.
    CAPTION: The reeds opened — and revealed it. (Turn the page.)

p8  CAPTION: The forest Vael had names for many things. They had no name for this.
    TITLE: the Marsh Vael

p9  MARSH VAEL: FIRE-PEOPLE... FIRE-PEOPLE CAME... FIRE-PEOPLE —
    SORREL: THEY'RE AFRAID OF US.
    ROOK: WE CAME OUT OF THE FIRE-DIRECTION. TO THEM, WE MIGHT BE THE THING THAT BURNS.
    CAPTION: MARSH-MOTHER BRACKEN. Straight-backed despite her age. Her face is a closed door.
    BRACKEN: SEDGE. YOU BROUGHT FIRE INTO MY REEDS.
    SEDGE: I BROUGHT VAEL, MARSH-MOTHER. LIVING VAEL. NOT THE LAST OF US AFTER ALL.

p10 BRACKEN: EVERY CHILD IN THIS MARSH IS RAISED ON ONE TRUTH. WE ARE THE LAST. WE SURVIVE
             BECAUSE WE TRUST NO ONE ELSE.
    BRACKEN: AND NOW A BOY WITH A CAT WANTS ME TO UNLEARN IT IN AN AFTERNOON.
    ROOK: I'M NOT ASKING YOU TO UNLEARN ANYTHING. I THOUGHT THE SAME, THREE DAYS AGO.
          THEN I FOUND YOU.
    ROOK: WE DON'T WANT YOUR WATER. WE WANT TO KNOW HOW FAR THE FIRE IS. AND WHETHER THERE
          ARE OTHERS — SKY, STONE, WHEREVER THE OLD STORIES SCATTERED US.
    BRACKEN: THE SCATTERING IS NOT A STORY FOR GUESTS. IT IS A WOUND. AND WOUNDS HAVE TWO SIDES.
    SEDGE: I DON'T KNOW WHAT SHE MEANS. SHE'S NEVER TOLD US EITHER.

p11 SCOUT: MARSH-MOTHER! THE EAST CHANNEL — SMOKE! SOMETHING'S CUTTING THE REEDS —
           CUTTING THEM STRAIGHT —
    BRACKEN: THE REEDS DON'T CUT STRAIGHT. NOTHING HERE CUTS STRAIGHT.
    ROOK: ...STRAIGHT. BRACKEN — DID YOU SAY STRAIGHT?
    ROOK: NO. NOTHING DOES. IT FOLLOWED US. IT'S HERE.

p12 ROOK: HOW MANY WAYS IN? CAN THEY BURN WET REED?
    SEDGE: GREEN REED WON'T TAKE FIRE FAST. BUT IF THEY CUT A STRAIGHT CHANNEL, THEY DRAIN
           THE LAGOON — AND THEN IT WILL.
    ROOK: THEN WE DON'T LET THEM CUT. YOUR PEOPLE KNOW THE WATER. MINE KNOW HOW TO FIGHT
          WHAT COMES ON LAND.
    SEDGE: YOU'VE KNOWN US SINCE BREAKFAST AND YOU WANT TO BLEED FOR OUR REEDS?
    ROOK: I WANT TO NOT BE ALONE AGAIN. THAT MEANS THE REEDS ARE WORTH BLEEDING FOR.

p13 CAPTION: They did not shout. They did not rage. They simply advanced, and the world went
             straight where they passed.
    CAPTION: Rook signed a plan with his hands. Forest and marsh, crouched in the reeds, unseen.
    SEDGE: ON YOUR WORD, FIRE-BOY.
    ROOK: NOW.

p14 SFX: KRRSH   HSSSS
    CAPTION: Back to back. Bone knife and reed-spear. Covering each other without needing to speak.
    CAPTION: It did not fight like a person. It fought like a tool redirected.
    SEDGE: THEY DON'T FLINCH! THEY DON'T EVEN LOOK AT YOU — THEY LOOK THROUGH YOU —
    ROOK: I KNOW. YARROW WARNED ME. KEEP THEM OFF THE MACHINE!

p15 CAPTION: Unable to advance in the tangled wet, they simply disengaged — retreating in a
             straight line, taking their dead without a word.
    ROOK: LET THEM GO. WE WON.
    SEDGE: THAT WASN'T WINNING. THAT WAS THEM DECIDING TO STOP.
    CAPTION: A thing of straight iron in a world of curves. Deeply wrong.
    ROOK: ...THEN WE'D BETTER LEARN EVERYTHING WE CAN BEFORE THEY DECIDE TO START AGAIN.

p16 SEDGE: YOU FOUGHT FOR REEDS YOU SAW FOR THE FIRST TIME THIS MORNING.
    ROOK: IF THERE ARE TWO OF US, SEDGE, THERE ARE MORE. SKY. STONE. WHEREVER THE SCATTERING
          SENT US.
    SEDGE: ...THEN YOU'LL NEED SOMEONE WHO KNOWS THE WATER BETWEEN HERE AND THE MOUNTAINS.
    SEDGE: I'M SAYING YOU'D DIE WITHOUT ME. SAME THING.
    SEDGE: ...RILL LIKES YOU. RILL HAS TERRIBLE JUDGMENT. BUT SHE'S NEVER WRONG.
    SORREL: MOTH, STOP, YOU CAN'T BE JEALOUS OF A SNAKE —

p17 YARROW: YOUR PEOPLE FIGHT WELL, MARSH-MOTHER. ODD, HOW THAT ONLY MATTERED ONCE WE STOOD
            ON THE SAME SIDE.
    BRACKEN: DON'T MISTAKE ONE FIGHT FOR TRUST, FOREST-CRONE.
    YARROW: I WOULDN'T DREAM OF IT. TRUST TAKES LONGER. I ONLY NOTE WE'RE ALL STILL ALIVE
            TO ARGUE ABOUT IT.
    BRACKEN: ...THAT KNIFE.

p18 BRACKEN: WHERE DID YOU GET THAT BLADE. THE BONE ONE. AT YOUR BELT.
    ROOK: IT WAS MY MOTHER'S. AND HERS BEFORE THAT. WHY?
    BRACKEN: TURN IT. THE HEEL OF THE HANDLE. THERE'S A MARK CUT INTO THE BONE.
    ROOK: ...THERE'S A MARK. I NEVER KNEW WHAT IT MEANT.
    BRACKEN: IT'S THE MARK OF THE MARSH VAEL. THAT IS OUR BONE. OUR MAKING.
    BRACKEN: A FOREST CHIEF SHOULD NOT BE CARRYING IT. UNLESS A FOREST CHIEF TOOK IT.

p19 ROOK: I DON'T — MY MOTHER GAVE IT TO ME. I DON'T KNOW HOW SHE —
    BRACKEN: LONG AGO THE FOREST VAEL AND THE MARSH WERE ONE PEOPLE. AND THE FOREST TOOK
             SOMETHING FROM US, AND LEFT, AND LET US BELIEVE WE WERE ALONE FOR IT.
    BRACKEN: NO. YOU WOULDN'T. THEY NEVER TELL THE CHILDREN THE SHAMEFUL PARTS.
    BRACKEN: AND NOW THEIR CHIEF WALKS IN WEARING THE PROOF AT HIS BELT AND ASKS ME TO GATHER.
    CAPTION: The two tribes pulled apart. Rook stood alone in the middle, the knife in his hand.

p20 SEDGE: MARSH-MOTHER. HE DIDN'T KNOW. YOU CAN SEE HE DIDN'T KNOW.
    BRACKEN: WHAT HE KNEW CHANGES NOTHING ABOUT WHAT WAS DONE.
    ROOK: I CAN'T ANSWER FOR WHAT MY PEOPLE DID BEFORE I WAS BORN. I WON'T PRETEND I CAN.
    ROOK: BUT I'M HOLDING IT NOW. SO TELL ME WHAT WAS TAKEN. AND I'LL HELP YOU MAKE IT
          RIGHT — IF MAKING IT RIGHT IS POSSIBLE.
    CAPTION: A long, terrible silence across the water.

p21 BRACKEN: YOU WANT TO MAKE IT RIGHT.
    BRACKEN: THEN I WILL TELL YOU WHAT WAS TAKEN. AND YOU WILL NOT LIKE WHERE IT LEADS.
    BRACKEN: THE FOREST DIDN'T ONLY TAKE A BLADE, FOREST-CHIEF.
    BRACKEN: THEY TOOK THE FIRST OF US. THE ONE WHO REMEMBERED THE SKY BEFORE IT BROKE.
    CAPTION: The Riven Sky mystery and the tribal wound, colliding into one.
