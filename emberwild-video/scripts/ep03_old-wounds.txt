Two tribes who each thought they were the last. One night
standing together. One knife to undo it all.




                                                                No one had crossed the water since Bracken spoke.




Rook held it out until his legs shook, waiting for someone to
take it, or name it. No one had.

                                                                            3
Both tribes watched them go — forest and
marsh in one boat, for the first time.




BRAM HALF OUR FIGHTERS, WALKING
AWAY FROM THE ONLY WATER FOR A
STORY. IF THE IRONFOLK COME BACK
—




                                           ROOK THEN THE TRIBES DEFEND EACH
                                           OTHER, OR THEY DON'T. THAT'S THE
                                           THING I NEED TO KNOW BEFORE I ASK
                                           ANYONE TO GATHER.




                                           SEDGE ...THEN I'M IN THE BOAT.
                                           SOMEONE HAS TO KEEP THE FIRE-CHIEF
                                           FROM GETTING EATEN BY A MOOR.




                                                                                4
They left the water by midday. The reeds thinned to
grass, and the grass to bone-pale stalks that hissed in a
wind with nothing to stop it.




SORREL I DON'T LIKE IT UP HERE.
NOTHING TO HIDE UNDER. THE
SKY'S JUST... WATCHING.




YARROW THE SKY ISN'T WATCHING,
CHILD. THE SKY IS BROKEN. IT
STOPPED WATCHING ANYTHING
LONG AGO. THAT'S THE WHOLE
TROUBLE.




                                                            5
No trees for a proper fire. Yarrow coaxed a
small blaze in a stone hollow, and the truth
began.




BRACKEN WE WERE ONE PEOPLE.                        YARROW OUR SONGS SAY THE
FOREST AND MARSH, BEFORE                           FOREST VAEL WERE ALWAYS OF
EITHER HAD A NAME. WE LIVED                        THE TREES.
HERE, UNDER THE RINGS, WHEN
THE SKY WAS WHOLE.




                                                                 WREN THE CORD SAYS
                                                                 OTHERWISE, KEEPER. IT SAYS YOU
                                                                 LEFT THE RINGS. I'M SORRY.




 YARROW ...GO ON, THEN. BOTH OF
 YOU. I'LL LISTEN TO THE KNOTS.




                                               BRACKEN WHEN THE SKY BROKE, THE
                                               BONDS BEGAN TO FAIL. WE STARTED TO
                                               LOSE EACH OTHER.




                                                                                                  6
 The elders were afraid. A people whose souls come in
 halves, and the halves stop arriving — that's a people
 with a counted end.




                                                                 SEDGE SO THE FIRST WASN'T JUST
                                                                 SOMEONE YOU LOVED. THE FIRST
                                                                 WAS A PLACE THE BONDS STILL
                                                                 WORKED.




One among us remembered the sky
before it broke. Where the First stood,
the bonds held. Every time.




                                                          BRACKEN THE FIRST WAS BOTH.
                                                          THAT'S WHAT MADE THE NEXT PART
                                                          UNFORGIVABLE.




  BRACKEN THE FOREST GREW
  AFRAID THE MOOR WAS DYING.
  THEY DECIDED TO LEAVE FOR THE
  TREES.




                                                                 BRACKEN AND THEY DID NOT ASK TO
                                                                 TAKE THE FIRST. THEY TOOK. IN THE
                                                                 NIGHT. WITH A BLADE CUT FROM THE
                                                                 FIRST'S OWN PEOPLE'S BONE.




                                                                                                     7
The forest Vael sat with an ancestral crime named
aloud. Sedge watched Rook's face — not gloating. Just
watching.




                                       YARROW ...IS THERE A KNOT THAT SAYS
                                       WE ASKED. THAT WE BEGGED, AND
                                       WERE REFUSED. ANY KNOT THAT MAKES
                                       IT SMALLER.




WREN THERE'S A KNOT FOR HUNGER,
KEEPER. YOUR PEOPLE WERE
STARVING TOO. THE CORD DOESN'T
FORGIVE IT. BUT BOTH SIDES WERE
DYING. THAT'S THE PART THE MARSH-
MOTHER'S TELLING LEAVES OUT.




                                                               Bracken's jaw tightened. Corrected, out loud,
                                                               exactly as ordered.



                                                                                                               8
BRACKEN I RAISED HER TO CORRECT
ME. I DID NOT THINK IT WOULD FEEL
LIKE THAT.




                                        YARROW IT FELT TRUE. THAT'S
                                        USUALLY THE ONES THAT HURT.




BRACKEN THE THEFT DIDN'T COST US
THE FIRST. WE GRIEVED THE FIRST
AND LIVED. IT COST US THE SKY.




                                    BRACKEN OUR BONDS GOT HARDER.
                                    SLOWER. WREN IS NINETEEN AND WAITS
                                    STILL.




ROOK OURS COME EASY. THREE
NIGHTS. A CHILD ASKS AND THE
FIRE ANSWERS.




                                      BRACKEN BECAUSE YOU TOOK THE
                                      THING THAT MAKES THEM ANSWER.
                                      YOUR EASY BONDINGS ARE OUR
                                      STOLEN ONES.




                                                                         9
BRACKEN I CAN'T GIVE THE FIRST
BACK. THE FIRST IS BONES AND
CENTURIES BY NOW.




                                                   ROOK THEN WHAT DOES MAKING IT
                                                   RIGHT MEAN, BRACKEN? TELL ME THE
                                                   REAL THING YOU WANT, NOT THE KNIFE.




BRACKEN I WANT TO KNOW IF IT'S ONLY
THE FIRST. OR IF THE REASON THE
WHOLE WORLD'S SOULS COME BROKEN
IS BIGGER THAN ONE THEFT.




                                                  BRACKEN THE FIRST REMEMBERED THE
                                                  SKY WHOLE. FIND HOW THE SKY BROKE
                                                  — AND MAYBE THE BONDS COULD BE
                                                  WHOLE. FOR EVERYONE.




                                      BRACKEN THE GATHERING ISN'T ABOUT
                                      NUMBERS. IT'S ABOUT FINDING
                                      WHOEVER REMEMBERS THE SKY.
                                      PIECING THE MEMORY BACK TOGETHER.




BRACKEN NOW YOU UNDERSTAND
WHAT YOU VOLUNTEERED FOR. IT'S
WHY I SAID YOU WOULDN'T LIKE
WHERE IT LEADS.



                                                                                         10
The Rings. Where the Vael were one — and stopped
being one. The broken sky hung directly above the
broken circle of stone.




SORREL ROOK — MOTH WON'T
SETTLE. I FEEL — LIKE THE BOND'S
LOUDER HERE. LIKE MOTH'S
CLOSER THAN SHE'S EVER BEEN.




 ROOK THAT'S IT. THE BONDS HOLD
 STRONGER INSIDE THE RINGS. IT'S
 FADED — BUT IT'S STILL HERE.




                                                    SEDGE NINETEEN YEARS, I'VE NEVER
                                                    FELT RILL THIS CLEAR. WREN — STAND
                                                    IN THE CENTER. JUST TRY.

THERE?

                                                 HERE.

The ground only remembers a little. So we

                                                                                      15
Bracken, Wren, Sorrel and Moth fled with the cords.
Rook, Sedge, Bram and Yarrow stayed to delay the
column at the Rings.




SEDGE I KNOW THE WATER BETWEEN
HERE AND THE MOUNTAINS, I SAID. I
SAID NOTHING ABOUT FIGHTING IRON
ON A BALD HILL WITH NO WATER.




                                                      BRAM YOU VOLUNTEERED. — I SAID
                                                      YOU'D DIE WITHOUT ME. KEEPING MY
                                                      WORD. LOUDLY RESENTING IT.




YARROW LET THEM COME TO THE
RINGS, THEN. THIS GROUND MADE
BONDS WHEN THE WORLD WAS YOUNG.
LET'S SEE HOW IT LIKES IRON.




                                                                                         16
They did not slow at the stones. To them a Ring was a
shape, and shapes were only things to make straight.




                            KR R S H




SEDGE ROOK — THE STONES —
RILL'S STRONGER IN HERE, SHE
SHOULDN'T BE ABLE TO—




                  ROOK THEN WE DON'T LEAVE THE
                  CIRCLE. HOLD THE CENTER!




                                                        17
One figure — taller, its mask marked differently — stopped.
And turned its blank face slowly across the Rings. Not like
a tool. Like something looking.




ROOK ...THAT ONE'S LOOKING. THEY
DON'T LOOK. SEDGE — THAT ONE IS
LOOKING AT THE STONES THE WAY WE
DO.




The marked one raised a hand — and the whole column
stopped at once, and withdrew in a straight line. Not
defeated. Recalled.




                                                              SEDGE THAT'S NOT A SOLDIER.
                                                              THAT'S A—




                                                                                            18
It was the last to leave. And it paused at the circle's
edge — to look back. Once.




ROOK IT LOOKED BACK. AT THE STONES.
A TOOL DOESN'T GRIEVE A THING IT'S
LEAVING.




                                                          YARROW NO. IT DOESN'T.




SEDGE YOU ASKED HOW THE IRONFOLK KNOW
WHAT THE RINGS ARE. WHAT IF THEY KNOW
BECAUSE THEY WERE HERE. WHAT IF THE
STRAIGHT-LINE PEOPLE WERE VAEL ONCE TOO —
THE ONES WHOSE BONDS FAILED HARDEST,
WHOSE HALVES NEVER CAME.




                                                                                   19
ROOK ...A VAEL WITH NO OTHER HALF
LEFT. NOTHING TO BOND TO.




                                                   SEDGE SO THEY BONDED TO IRON
                                                   INSTEAD. TO THE STRAIGHT LINE. TO
                                                   THE THING THAT DOESN'T NEED A SOUL.




The forest-chief kept his word. He didn't die on
her stones.




 BRACKEN YOU KEPT
 IT.




                                                   ROOK I KEEP MY WORD. IT'S THE ONLY
                                                   THING I'VE GOT THAT ISN'T STOLEN.




                                                                                         20
BRACKEN THE WOUND'S STILL A
WOUND. BUT A WOUND WITH TWO
PEOPLE HOLDING IT CLOSED BLEEDS
SLOWER THAN ONE. WE'LL GATHER.




                                                 BRACKEN ON ONE CONDITION. —
                                                 NAME IT.




BRACKEN WHEN WE FIND THEM — THE SKY
ONES, THE STONE ONES, WHOEVER'S LEFT —
NO ONE TAKES ANYONE. EVER AGAIN. WE ASK.
ESPECIALLY WHEN WE'RE DYING.




                                                 ROOK ON THE FIRST'S CORD. WE
                                                 ASK.




SORREL IT'S NOT FAIR. THAT MINE
CAME IN THREE NIGHTS AND YOURS
WON'T COME AT ALL.




                                           SORREL MOTH LIKES YOU. SHE KEEPS
                                           LOOKING AT YOU. MAYBE SOME HALVES
                                           ARE JUST... PEOPLE.




                                                                                21
 All slept but Rook, who kept watch where the
 Ironfolk had gone.




ROOK ...I'M NOT GOING TO HURT
YOU. WE CAME LOOKING FOR YOU.




STRANGER I KNOW. I'VE BEEN
FOLLOWING THE STRAIGHT LINE
TOO.




YOU'RE TOO LATE FOR THE
MOUNTAIN. THE IRONFOLK ALREADY
HAVE THE ONE WHO REMEMBERS
THE SKY.
