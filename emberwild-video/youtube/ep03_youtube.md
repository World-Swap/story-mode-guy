THE WORLD

Every Vael is born half a soul. The other half comes to them through fire, or through water,
and joins with them for life. The bond cannot be faked — the creatures cannot abide a liar —
so only the honest are ever truly chosen.

Overhead hangs the Riven Sky, a vast shattered ring of pale light arcing across the world day
and night. No one alive remembers it whole. This is the episode where the saga stops treating
that as scenery and starts treating it as a crime scene.

The Vael world is made of curves — rivers, roots, hair, horns, the broken ring itself. The one
thing that draws a straight line is the enemy. On this moor, for the first time, the two shapes
