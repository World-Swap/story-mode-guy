## 3 · The break: Episode 3 → Episode 4

### 🔴 A. Wren is two different people

| Ep3 (canon) | Ep4 (mine) |
|---|---|
| **19 years old** | 13 years old |
| **Marsh Vael**, raised by Bracken | Sky Clan sentinel |
| **Unbonded** — the ache of the issue | Bonded to storm-bird **Tern** |
| Keeps the **knot-cords** (the marsh's records) | Rides, carries a spear |

Ep3 builds her whole arc on the wait: *"Wren is nineteen and waits still,"* then she stands in the Rings, asks, and **nothing comes** — *"...no. Not even here."* My Ep4 hands her a storm-bird and makes her thirteen, which erases it.

**Fix:** rename the Sky Clan youth (suggest **Kite**, **Lark**, or **Swift**) and give the storm-bird Tern to her. Wren stays marsh, 19, unbonded, cord-keeper — and should be *in the travelling party* from Ep3 onward, since she carries the cords.
**Art impact:** Ep4 illustrated pp. 3–4 (all Wren-rider panels) and the Ep4 design sheet must be redrawn/relabelled.

### 🔴 B. Episode 4's title and premise are overwritten by Ep3's ending

Ep3's last page states, on the record:
- *"**Next: Episode Four — The Sky Cage.**"*
- *"You're **too late for the mountain**. The Ironfolk **already have** the one who remembers the sky."*

My Ep4 is titled *The Sky Clan* and shows the Sky Clan free, proud, and refusing to join, with Halcyon safe and delivering lore at leisure. That's the story Ep3 explicitly forecloses.

**Fix:** Ep4 becomes **"The Sky Cage"** — the sky-rememberer is *already captive*; the peaks are compromised; this is a rescue, not a recruitment.

### 🟠 C. The Ironfolk-were-Vael reveal is spent twice

Ep3 pp. 19–20 already lands it, as Rook and Sedge's own deduction:
> *"What if the straight-line people were Vael once too — the ones whose bonds failed hardest, whose halves never came." / "So they bonded to iron instead."*

My Ep5 p17 replays it as Ash's fresh bombshell. It can't be a revelation twice.

**Fix:** Ep5's Ash **confirms it from the inside** rather than discovering it — and adds what Ep3 *couldn't* know: the mechanism (the bond is *cut out* of children), the castes (cutters / wardens / the will at the centre), the Forge, and Vane. Theory → eyewitness. That's a stronger scene anyway.

### 🟠 D. The Rings vanish — and they're the quest engine

Ep3 establishes:
- The Rings: bonds **hold stronger** inside the stone circle. Faded, but real.
- *"The ground only remembers a little. So we find where it remembers more."*
- Bracken: *"The gathering isn't about numbers. It's about **finding whoever remembers the sky**. Piecing the memory back together."*

My Ep4 never mentions any of it, and reframes the gathering as recruitment — Rook literally asks for *"your wings. Your eyes. Your high ground."* That's a thematic regression from Ep3.

**Fix:** Ep4's goal is **memory, not manpower**. Rook climbs because the Sky Clan looks up — they may hold a piece of the sky's memory.

### 🟠 E. The pact is never paid off

Ep3 ends on a sworn oath on the First's cord:
> *"When we find them — the sky ones, the stone ones, whoever's left — **no one takes anyone. Ever again. We ask.** Especially when we're dying."*

This is the moral spine handed forward, and neither Ep4 nor Ep5 names it. Worse — it's *already being paid off* accidentally:
- Ep4: Rook **asks** Kestrel instead of coercing → should be explicitly because he swore it.
- Ep5: Rook refuses to hand Ash back → *"we don't give a person to the fire to spare ourselves the burn"* is **exactly** the pact under maximum pressure (the enemy, when they're dying). Name it.

**Fix:** make the pact explicit in both. It's free thematic payoff sitting on the table.

### 🟡 F. Bracken and Wren are missing from Ep4

Ep3's party at close: Rook, Sedge, Bram, Yarrow, **Bracken**, **Wren**, Sorrel, Moth, Cinder. Bracken swears *"We'll gather."* Wren carries the cords.

My Ep4's party: Rook, Cinder, Sorrel, Moth, Bram, Thornclaw, Yarrow, Sedge — **no Bracken, no Wren, no cords.**

**Fix:** include them, or give an on-page reason they stayed with the marsh. The cords are the surviving record of the sky — they can't just evaporate.

### 🟡 G. Unanswered Ep3 questions Ep5 is perfectly placed to answer

Ep3 asks, and nobody ever answers:
> *"How do the Ironfolk know what the Rings are? They don't bond."*
> *"What do they want with a place that mends souls?"*

**Fix:** Ash answers this in Ep5. It's the best possible use of her.

### 🟡 H. Ep4's opening ignores Ep3 entirely

Ep4 p1 captions read as if following Ep2 straight on: *"Two tribes walked together now. Fire and water."* No moor, no Rings, no oath, no warning from the stranger.

**Fix:** re-caption the splash to follow the moor and the stranger's warning.

### ❓ I. Open question — who is the stranger on Ep3 p22?

A hooded figure with one visible eye: *"I know. I've been following the straight line too."* Rook says *"We came looking for you."*

Ep5 currently assumes the tribes have **never** heard an Ironfolk speak — if this stranger *is* Ironfolk, that's contradicted. **This is your call and it gates the Ep4 rewrite:**
1. **A Sky Clan survivor / scout** — fled the cage, guides them to the mountain. (Cleanest; keeps Ep5's "never taken one alive" intact.)
2. **Ash, arriving two issues early** — then Ep5 must be re-planned and the first-contact framing dropped.
3. **A new fourth-tribe character (stone?)** — opens Act II wider.

## 4 · Recommended fix order

1. **Decide who the Ep3 stranger is** (gates everything).
2. **Rewrite Ep4 as "The Sky Cage"** — rescue, not recruitment. Rename the Sky Clan youth; restore Wren (19, marsh, cords); bring Bracken; name the pact; goal = memory.
3. **Revise Ep5** — Ash confirms rather than reveals; she answers the Rings question; the pact is tested on the enemy.
4. **Update the Series Bible** — Ep4 title, Wren's real profile, the Rings, the First, the cords, the pact, the Ironfolk theory.
5. **Redo Ep3's art** in the colour style so all five match.
6. **Then** extend all issues from 4 illustrated pages toward the full 22.

## 5 · Canon locked by Episode 3 (do not contradict)

- **The Rings** — broken stone circle on the moor; the Vael were one people there; bonds still hold stronger inside; the broken sky hangs directly above the broken circle.
- **The First** — the one who remembered the sky whole. *Where the First stood, the bonds held.* Both a beloved person **and** a place the bonds worked.
- **The theft** — the forest, starving and afraid the moor was dying, **took** the First in the night, with a blade cut from the marsh's own bone (Rook's knife). Both sides were starving — the cord records hunger, and does not forgive it.
- **The cost** — not the First, but *the sky*. Marsh bonds went slow (Wren, 19, still waiting); forest bonds come in three nights. *"Your easy bondings are our stolen ones."*
- **Wren** — 19, unbonded, keeps the knot-cords, raised by Bracken, ordered to correct her out loud.
- **Records** — forest keeps memory in smoke and song; marsh keeps it in knots. *"Same wound, two ways."*
- **The Ironfolk theory** — Vael whose bonds failed hardest, whose halves never came; bonded to iron instead. One of them **looked** at the Rings, and **looked back** — *"a tool doesn't grieve a thing it's leaving."*
- **The marked one** — taller, differently-marked mask; raised a hand and recalled the whole column. Not a soldier.
- **The pact** — sworn on the First's cord: *no one takes anyone, ever again. We ask.*
- **The real goal** — find whoever remembers the sky; piece the memory back together; maybe make the bonds whole for everyone.
- **Ep3's hook** — *"You're too late for the mountain. The Ironfolk already have the one who remembers the sky."* → **Episode Four — The Sky Cage.**
