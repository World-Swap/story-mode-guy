## Canon the video pipeline must not break

- **Iron Warden Vane's full reveal is held for Ep6.** Any earlier Ironfolk on screen is an
  anonymous helm. (An Ep2 shot violated this once and was re-rolled.)
- **Wren is 19, marsh, unbonded, keeper of the knot-cords.** Not Sky Clan, not 13, not bonded.
- **The Sky Clan youth is Kite**, bonded to the storm-bird Tern.
- **The world is curves; the Ironfolk are the only straight lines.** This is the visual spine.
- **The Pact** — *"no one takes anyone. Ever again. We ask."* — is the moral spine from Ep3 on.
